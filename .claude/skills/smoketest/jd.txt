Senior Product Manager, AI Onboarding (Copenhagen, hybrid)

- Shipped at least one ML or LLM feature to production, with measured outcomes
- Experience in a regulated industry (financial services, healthcare or pharma)
- Comfortable with discovery: interviews, prototypes, experiments with quantified results
- Line management of product managers (you will hire two)
- Fluent English; Danish is a plus
- Based in or willing to relocate to Copenhagen, 3 days a week on site

Nice to have
- B2B or SME banking background
- Experience with growth loops and activation metrics
